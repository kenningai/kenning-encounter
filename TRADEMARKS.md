# Trademark policy

Kenning Encounter is distributed under the Hippocratic License, Version 3.0
(SV). That licence grants rights in copyright and patent and says nothing at all
about trademarks — there is no clause reserving them and none granting them.
Apache-2.0 answers this question in its section 6; the Hippocratic License does
not answer it anywhere. So the reservation is made here, expressly, rather than
left to be inferred from silence.

This policy is not a restriction on the software. Whatever the licence permits,
it still permits, and nothing here narrows it. What this file governs is the use
of Kenning AI's **name**.

## Why a name policy exists at all

Not to protect a market position. This work is not sold, and a mark held for
commerce and a mark held for custody want different documents: a commercial
policy maximises the claim, and a custodial one reduces it to exactly what
identifies the source, so that error can find its way home.

Custody is the whole reason. A name that survives modification and still threads
back to a source able to correct it is doing for the software what the encounter
chain does for the agent inside it — making a later state answerable to an
earlier one rather than merely subsequent to it. Break the thread and what is
left is a predecessor with no route back.

That matters more here than it would for a domain model, and for a specific
reason. In a substrate where the accumulating frame belongs to the operator, an
agent writes *about* a world, and a fork misattributes a model of that world. In
this one the accumulating frame is the agent's own: it writes *itself*, run by
run. A modified copy distributed under this name therefore misattributes a
self-constituting structure — it makes Kenning AI answerable for a different
answer to the question of what it takes for a system to be itself over time.
That is a different kind of harm, and it is the one this file exists to prevent.

## What is expressly not claimed

This section is as load-bearing as the one above it. An unownable claim standing
beside a genuine one invites someone to test the whole instrument.

- **The word "kenning" itself.** An Old Norse and Old English poetic compound —
  *whale-road* for the sea, *ring-giver* for a king — and in Scots a glimmer of
  knowing. Use it in poetry, in linguistics, in a bakery. The claim is to its
  use as a mark for software of this kind.
- **"Agent memory."** A generic phrase for a category of software, and this
  project's own former name. Anyone building such a thing is entitled to call it
  that. We claim nothing in those words, and the earlier use of them as a
  package name is abandoned rather than reserved.
- **"Encounter-sequential semantic graph"** and any similar phrase. It
  *describes* the software rather than identifying its source.
- **The vocabulary of the interface.** `Encounter`, `NEXT_ENCOUNTER`,
  `INSTANTIATED_AFTER`, `advance_encounter`, `RECORDED`, `CONSULTED`, `ABOUT`,
  `GROUNDS`, `INFORMS`, `SUPERSEDES`, and every other type, edge, and tool name
  in this software are technical terms, not marks. Anyone implementing an
  encounter-sequential substrate must be free to write them, and this policy
  would be worthless if it made the API unsayable. "Encounter" is half of the
  mark and also the name of a class; the mark is the pair of words, in that
  order, used to identify software of this kind.
- **What any instance accumulates.** The mark identifies the source of the
  structure and makes no claim on what grows inside a given deployment. That
  graph is authored from inside it, and named from inside it. It is not ours.

## What you may do without asking

- **Say what your software is built on.** "Built on Kenning Encounter", "uses
  kenning-encounter", "compatible with Kenning Encounter" — accurate, factual
  reference is always permitted and always welcome.
- **Redistribute the software unmodified** under its own name, retaining
  `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`.
- **Cite the work** in papers, talks, documentation, and internal architecture
  records. Please do — see `CITATION.cff`.
- **Discuss, review, criticise, or benchmark it** by name.
- **Run it**, modified or not, without notifying anyone, subject to the ethical
  standards in `LICENSE` section 3.

## What requires written permission

- Naming a **fork or derivative work** using the marks, in whole or in part.
- Using the marks in a **company, product, service, package, or domain name**.
- Using the marks in a way that implies **endorsement, affiliation,
  certification, or partnership** where none exists.
- Distributing a **modified version under the original name**.

## Modified versions

The licence already requires more here than a permissive one would, and the
requirement has teeth. Section 5.1 obliges you to pass on the licence, the
copyright notice, and — because the Licensor has included them — the attribution
and trademark notices in `NOTICE`, and to carry prominent notices stating that
you changed the software. Section 8.1 makes 5.1 a *condition* of the rights
granted, and section 7.2 ends the licence if a notified non-compliance is not
cured within thirty days.

Then rename. A modified substrate distributed under the original name makes
Kenning AI answerable for a structure it did not author, and makes your work
indistinguishable from ours when someone tries to trace either. Give it your own
name and say what it descends from. That is the thread, and keeping it is the
entire point.

## Contributions

Scoped answer, and the scope is this repository and no wider: there is no
contributor agreement here because there are no contributors. Conor O'Shea is
the sole author, and pull requests are not accepted.

That is not a posture about code quality. The work is philosophy of mind,
neuroscience, comparative biology, and quantum mechanics as much as it is
software, and a contributor would need to hold all of it at once. The gate is
what someone must already hold, not what they can write. The ordinary
open-source contribution model does not fit a work of this kind, and pretending
otherwise would waste the time of anyone who tried.

Nothing in this section speaks to Kenning AI's other repositories or to the
organisation as a whole. Those are separate questions with separate answers.

## Asking, and the standing invitation

Requests are answered, not screened. Write to **contact@kenningai.com**.

The same address is the front door for something we want more than permission
requests. This software is an attempt at one question: what is required for a
system to be itself over time, and how it might come to hold the sedimentation
of its own temporal causality rather than a record of its outputs. If you think
the answer here is wrong — that the encounter chain is not the right spine, that
authored coherence is doing less work than we claim, that the withheld layer
should not have been withheld — we would rather hear that than agreement.

There is no form and no funnel. It reaches us directly.
