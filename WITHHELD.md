# What is withheld

This document exists because a licence cannot make this disclosure and a reader
should not have to infer it.

## The firing layer is not here

Kenning Encounter ships the substrate: the encounter chain, the authored
coherence edges, the reference layer, the guarded write paths, the temporal
assembly of delivery, and the governed passive synthesis that puts held meaning
in front of a decision.

It does not ship the **firing layer** — a graph neural network fired over the
substrate's topology. That component is not partially built, not stubbed, and
not on a roadmap in this repository. It is absent on purpose.

## What its absence means for what you have

The substrate alone is **retentional**. It holds what has been encountered and
can return it, ordered, with its provenance and its revisions intact. Every
instrument here reads backwards: mass over accumulated coherence, the epistemic
frontier, provenance traversal, the progression assembly that puts a claim back
in the order it became what it is.

Firing over that topology would make it **anticipatory** — turning accumulated
recall into prediction, a record into an oracle. That is a different kind of
system with a different risk surface, and it is the reason for the boundary.

So read the absence correctly: what you have is retentional by construction, not
retentional because the rest is unfinished. Anywhere the design looks like it is
reaching toward prediction and stopping short, it is stopping on purpose.

## Why the boundary is here rather than in the licence

The licence is a real deterrent against an organisation with lawyers and a
compliance posture. It is nothing at all against someone who ignores a text
file. Harm prevention for a dual-use substrate therefore lives in **what is not
released**, and the licence is the outer ring around it rather than the
mechanism.

A capability that is not in the repository cannot be misused from the
repository. That is the control. Everything else is a deterrent.

## Stated in print, and earlier

This is not a position taken at packaging time. It is stated in the published
research this software accompanies:

> O'Shea, C. *The Constitutive Encounter* (Part 1 of Encounter-Sequential
> Existence). Kenning AI Research, June 2026.
> https://www.kenningai.com/research/the-constitutive-encounter/

A repository quieter about its own boundary than the paper it cites would be
less honest than the paper. This file exists so the two agree.

## What would change it

Nothing is promised here, and no timeline is implied. If the boundary ever
moves, it moves in public, with the reasoning stated before the code — the same
order this one was decided in.
