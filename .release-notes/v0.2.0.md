# v0.2.0 — grounding, provenance, and the epistemic frontier

Four additions that deepen the existing graph without shifting the ontology — drawn from a survey of graph-driven agent-memory systems, kept only where they fit the substrate's constitutive principles.

- **`GROUNDS` edge (`Observation → Concept`).** The provenance rung from a synthesized `Concept` down to the Observations that constitute it. Distinct in kind from `ABOUT` (which *concerns* a Concept) and `INFORMS` (Concept→Concept lateral influence) — a different legal shape means a different relation, so it earns its own edge rather than overloading `INFORMS`. Source-constrained to Concept only; no asymmetric-pair machinery.
- **Required `revision_why` on `SUPERSEDES`.** A supersession now records not just *that* understanding shifted but *why*. The property is required and validated non-empty by the guarded write — a bare supersession loses the delta and is rejected. The re-entry payload and `find_by_name` now surface authored edge properties, so the why travels with the edge.
- **`trace_provenance` read tool.** Walks a node's grounding subtree along the authored coherence edges (never the auto-written spine) out to a bounded depth, returning the reachable nodes and the edges among them, each node annotated with the Encounter that recorded it. A Concept resolves to its `GROUNDS` Observations and their Citations; a Hypothesis to what supports/challenges it; a Question to what raises/resolves it. Surfaces — never authors.
- **Epistemic frontier in `orient`.** Alongside the GDS spread, `orient` now returns where the topology is thinnest — unanswered Questions, untested Hypotheses, ungrounded Concepts, confidence/evidence dissonance (authored confidence outrunning structural support), and contested Hypotheses. Derived from existing structure, no new schema, no extra projection. Centrality says what you are; the frontier says where to look next.

Docs reconciled: the operations manual (HOWTO) and the architecture notes carry the new edge and tools, and the process-layer description is brought in line with the settled EERRS model (EERRS is one *existence*; an existence seals one or more Encounters). Internal project guidance updated alongside.
