# v0.6.0 — the progression reform: delivery becomes temporal

The largest change to the governed infusion since it shipped, closing a
failure class found live: two observations about the same subject, recorded
at different times and both true in their moment ("X is blocked by Y" /
"Y was fixed"), could be delivered side by side with no temporal ordering.
An LLM handed contradictory claims without their time relationship **must
invent one** to produce a coherent answer — confabulation as the necessary
consequence of tenseless delivery, not a model defect.

The repair splits the pipeline's two jobs cleanly:

- **Selection stays atemporal.** The coherence projection, biased rank, and
  frontier seeding are unchanged — they answer *what is relevant*.
- **Delivery becomes temporal.** A new assembly stage answers *in what order
  did this become what it is*.

## Progressions

For a selected observation whose subject is a bookmarked `Component`, a
two-hop fan-out collects the Component's sibling records (Observations,
Notes, Questions, Hypotheses alike) and delivers them as one **topic block**:

```
my-server — 9 steps, 03-01 → 03-06:
  → 03-01: constraint identified …  [seed]
  → 03-01: constraint resolved …
  → … 5 more steps folded
  NOW (03-06): current state, full body
      [GROUNDS → the concept this state evidences]
```

- Steps are ordered by noticing-time (`coalesce(t_observed, t_raised,
  t_proposed, t_created)`); every step renders **in its temporal place**.
- The **terminus** (current state) carries a full body plus its grounding
  edges inline; the **seed** step the ranking chose keeps a full body when
  distinct — one subject is not one state variable.
- Middle steps fold into a count line that is always true; blocks are
  **atomic** under the payload budget (kept whole or dropped whole — a
  mid-block cut could strand a stale claim without its resolution).
- Open Questions about the subject arrive **inside the block** — the
  frontier's voice in-topic — and are deduplicated from the OPEN THREADS
  register.

## The progression is the unit of renewal

First sight delivers the full block. Unchanged-and-fresh re-pins as one
`◦ progression standing` line naming the terminus. A new step delivers
`progression advanced (+N)` — terminus plus delta. A superseded claim can no
longer age out of the refresh window and return as a fresh standalone body
at full salience: progression members never flow through the standalone
path, in any form.

## Tense everywhere else

- Every standalone neighborhood body now carries an `[as of DATE]` label —
  a timestamp is knowledge; a disclaimer would be noise.
- Delta-mode recognitions arrive as *"step in progression X; current
  terminus: Y"* (or *"terminus of progression X"*), with the context inside
  the novelty fingerprint — a terminus advance is a new fact and
  re-announces once. Expect a one-time re-announce wave on upgrade.
- Observations that ground a Concept carry a one-line convergence
  annotation (principle + instance count, hard-capped).

## Accounting

The infuse result gains an `assembly` block: per-progression form and steps,
delivered bodies, and `unassembled` — selected observations with no
Component hub, i.e. **the working list for the new authoring discipline in
the HOWTO: bookmark anything whose state you will notice again.** The
progression form's coverage is bounded by that discipline. The result also
carries `server_version`, and the hook client logs both fields, so observe
logs can be stratified across the upgrade seam.

## Compatibility

Server-side only; hook wiring is unchanged (the hook client gains two logged
fields). The graph is untouched — no nodes or edges are written, modified,
or deleted by any of this; the assembly stage reads existing topology.
Restarting into v0.6.0 clears the in-process delivery ledger, so the first
turn of each session after upgrade re-delivers in full — expected, once.
